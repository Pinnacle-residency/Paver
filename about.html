Built for the People Other Apps Left Behind

Most financial apps are built for people who already have a stable financial foundation, a regular salary, a credit history, a bank account that's never overdrawn. Those apps work well for the people they were designed for.

What We Believe

Simplicity is Respect

We obsess over making every screen, and every action as simple as possible because you have enough to think about.

Guidance Over Data

Paver is designed to guide you and to suggest the next right step for you, not just display a number.

Built for Inclusion

Paver is built to feel familiar, trustworthy, and easy to use to people from every background in America.

Tolulope Ogunmakinwa is a fintech product strategist and technologist with experience designing and scaling digital financial products for underserved markets across Nigeria and the United States. She holds a Master of Science in Computer Information Systems from Prairie View A&M University with a 4.0 GPA, and professional certifications including PMP and PSM.

She is the founder of Paver, an AI-driven personal finance platform built specifically for unbanked and low-income populations in the United States. Her work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, behavioral design, and financial inclusion.
